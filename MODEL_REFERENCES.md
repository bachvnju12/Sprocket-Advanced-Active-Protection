# Model references

Seven models are photo-reference drafts, not measured hardware replicas. Drozd-2, Arena-M and Zaslin remain placeholders. Dimensions, hidden brackets and internals are estimates. Photographs stay local, are not texture pixels and are not packaged; access does not establish redistribution rights.

- Drozd-1: `T-55AD_with_Drozd_active_protection_system.jpg`. One cheek assembly: four canisters in paired rows, equipment housing and mounts. Radar/bracket dimensions remain approximate.
- Shtora-1: `T-90_Shtora-1_infrared_light_emitter.jpg`. One emitter, clipped housing, cooling fins/pins and protective bar. Reference has painted cover fitted; uncovered wine-red glossy IR-filter tint/depth are estimates. Housing uses native paint; glass is unpainted, without emission or beam.
- Trophy: `Trophy-APS--Merkava-4M-pic01-Zachi-Evenor_(cropped).jpg`. One side radar panel and adjacent launcher/shield. Covered internals and relative proportions remain draft.
- Afganit: [T-14 Armata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:T-14_Armata_(27053434067).jpg). Five low capped tubes, splayed side bank and rails; hidden mounts approximate.
- Iron Fist: [demonstration unit](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Iron_Fist_APS.jpg). Two stacked tubes, rotary yoke, pivot and base.
- Arena: [radar at Omsk](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arena_on_T-80UM-1_at_Omsk.png), [T-80UM-1 exhibition](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:T-80UM-1_at_VTTV-Omsk-2009_-01.jpg). One roof radar mast, not distributed countermeasure panels compressed into a disc.
- AMAP-ADS/StrikeShield: [Lynx exterior tile](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:StrikeShield_APS_on_Lynx_IFV.jpg). Paired recessed apertures; hidden internals omitted.

`generated/previews/` contains front/side/isometric views using runtime triangle winding, UV atlas and interpolated normals with depth/backface tests. Preview olive/sand colors do not demonstrate native camo; reflections are not HDRP. Catalog icons alone brighten filled RGB channels to >=150. Schema/winding/bounds/triangle tests do not measure photographic likeness. Game-scale visual/camo acceptance remains pending.
